# What Is Big Data?

Big data refers to datasets whose size, speed, or complexity exceed what traditional single-machine systems can process efficiently.

## Core Ideas

- Volume: large amounts of data.
- Velocity: data generated or processed quickly.
- Variety: structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data.
- Veracity: quality, consistency, and trustworthiness.
- Value: useful business or analytical outcomes.

## Why It Matters

Modern data systems use distributed storage and distributed compute to process data at scale.
