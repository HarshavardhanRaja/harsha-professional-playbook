## 📊 Ordering Logic

Platforms are ordered by:
1. **Monthly traffic in India** (estimated)
2. **Volume of Data Engineering roles posted**
3. **Recruiter activity & response rates** for DE positions

## 🧠 Strategy Tips (India-Specific)

1. **Referrals > Cold Apply:** For GCCs (JPMorgan, Goldman, Mercedes, Airbus) and top product companies, a referral is 3x more effective
2. **Update Naukri every 3-4 days** — small profile edits keep you at the top of recruiter search results
3. **Target GCC Belt:** Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune have the highest density of DE roles at Global Capability Centers
4. **Use XYZ formula in resume:** "Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z" (e.g., "Reduced pipeline latency by 30% by migrating batch to streaming")
5. **Apply within 48 hours** of a posting — roles posted >1 week ago have significantly lower response rates
6. **Stack-specific keywords:** Airflow, Snowflake, Databricks, dbt, Kafka, BigQuery, PySpark, GCP, AWS
