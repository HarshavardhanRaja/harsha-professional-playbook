## 🧠 Strategy Tips (International)

### Gulf / Middle East
1. **Tax-free salaries** are the #1 draw. Roles in Dubai/Abu Dhabi/Riyadh pay well with zero income tax
2. **Look for "visa sponsored" explicitly** in job descriptions
3. **UAE job-seeker visa** lets you search for work while in-country — consider for serious searches
4. **Focus on banking, oil & gas, smart city sectors** — these have the highest DE demand

### Europe
1. **Arbeitnow + GermanTechJobs** are best for English-speaking roles in Germany
2. **Honeypot is a game-changer** — companies apply to YOU, with visa support included
3. **EU Blue Card** is the key visa type — ensure roles qualify
4. **Netherlands, Germany, Ireland** have the strongest DE markets in Europe

### US / Canada
1. **H-1B sponsorship** — focus on companies known for sponsoring (Google, Meta, Amazon, fintech startups)
2. **O-1 visa** for extraordinary ability — if you have strong publications/open-source contributions
3. **Canadian PR pathway** is more accessible than US — consider Express Entry with LMIA-supported roles

### APAC
1. **Singapore Employment Pass** — requires minimum salary threshold. Focus on banks + tech companies
2. **Australia Skilled Worker visa (subclass 482)** — data engineering is on the skills shortage list

### General
1. **Resume format matters:** EU expects photo + date of birth (unlike US). Gulf expects years of experience prominently
2. **Highlight modern data stack:** Snowflake, Databricks, dbt, Airflow, Kafka, BigQuery — universally in demand
3. **Recruitment agencies are worth it** for international moves — they handle visa paperwork
