## 🧠 Strategy Tips (Remote-Specific)

1. **Check Remote Rocketship + HN Who's Hiring daily** — they surface jobs before LinkedIn
2. **Create one strong profile on YC Work at a Startup** — it distributes to hundreds of YC companies
3. **Set time-zone in Himalayas** — helps match you to companies in compatible time zones
4. **"Remote-first" > "Remote-friendly"** — remote-first companies have better infra for remote engineers
5. **Highlight async communication skills** — remote DE teams value documentation, self-driven work
6. **Showcase your stack prominently:** Python, SQL, Airflow, PySpark, Snowflake, BigQuery, MongoDB, AWS, GCP
7. **Use We Work Remotely + Remotive** as your "daily check" boards — highest signal, lowest noise
