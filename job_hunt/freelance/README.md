## 📊 Ordering Logic

Platforms are ordered by a combination of:
1. **Monthly site traffic** (estimated via SimilarWeb / industry reports)
2. **Volume of Data Engineering specific gigs/jobs**
3. **Recruiter/client activity** in the DE space

> ⚡ **Star ratings** = DE-specific relevance (not overall platform quality)
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ = Excellent for DE freelance work
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐ = Good, regular DE opportunities
> - ⭐⭐⭐ = Decent, but not DE-focused
> - ⭐⭐ = Low DE volume, but worth having a presence

## 🧠 Strategy Tips

1. **Start with vetted platforms** (Toptal, Arc.dev, Gun.io, Braintrust) — lower competition, higher rates
2. **Keep Upwork as backup** for building reviews and quick cash flow
3. **Use Contra + Malt** to build your personal brand with zero commission
4. **Apply to 3-5 new platforms per week** — don't try to do all 32 at once
5. **Track which platforms convert** — after 2-3 months, re-order this list based on YOUR results
6. **Showcase specific DE projects:** ETL pipelines, data platform migrations, RAG systems, dbt models
7. **Highlight your stack:** Python, SQL, Airflow, PySpark, Snowflake, BigQuery, MongoDB, AWS, GCP
